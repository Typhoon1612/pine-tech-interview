我想请教的三个问题

Three questions I would like to ask

01

目前有五个主体。如果同事已经使用了其中一个，而我对接的 Partner 也需要使用同一个主体，会发生冲突或覆盖吗？

There are currently five entities. If a colleague is already using one and the partner I am working with needs the same entity, could there be a conflict or would the existing assignment be overridden?

02

会议中提到「在哪一个国家开服务器」，是指 AWS Region 吗？

When someone asks which country the servers will run in, are they referring to the AWS Region?

03

会议中是否不可以提及主体或公司的名称？

Are we expected to avoid mentioning the entity or company name during meetings?

Q3 · 基础服务器 / 迁云Q3 · INFRASTRUCTURE / MIGRATION

我会如何回应 Partner

How I would respond to the partner

对方提问PARTNER'S QUESTION「要拿 MAP，是不是应该先把现有服务器迁到 AWS？你们要不要先购买我们的基础设施资源？」“To qualify for MAP, should you move your existing servers to AWS first? Would you consider purchasing our infrastructure resources?”
我的建议回应 · 中文

我们想听听贵方的专业意见：如果将基础设施一起迁移，整体上是否值得？是否可能获得更合适的 MAP credits 或 funding 支持？

能否请您提供书面建议，说明迁移范围、成本、预期收益及适用条件，方便我们内部评估？

目前我们希望先聚焦公司的当前方向：Claude on Amazon Bedrock。

MY PROPOSED RESPONSE · ENGLISH

We would value your advice on whether migrating the infrastructure alongside the AI workload would be worthwhile, and whether it could open up more suitable MAP credit or funding options.

Could you share a written recommendation covering the scope, costs, expected benefits and eligibility conditions for our internal review?

For now, we would like to focus on our current priority: Claude on Amazon Bedrock.

以上是建议话术。MAP 资格、credits 及 funding 均需进一步确认。Proposed wording. MAP eligibility, credits and funding remain subject to confirmation.

Q4 / Q5 / Q26 / Q28 · 通用处理建议Q4 / Q5 / Q26 / Q28 · SHARED APPROACH

让书面建议更方便比较

Make the written proposals easier to compare

我建议请 Partner 按相同项目整理方案，让内部团队更容易判断是否值得继续讨论。I would ask partners to cover the same points so our internal team can decide which suggestions deserve further discussion.

建议内容Recommendation

具体建议做什么？要解决哪个问题？What do you recommend, and which problem would it solve?

范围与依赖Scope and dependencies

与当前 Claude on Bedrock 计划有什么关系？哪些是必需，哪些是可选？How does it relate to our Claude on Bedrock plan? What is required and what is optional?

成本与支持Costs and support

预计成本是多少？可能有哪些 credits 或 funding？需要满足什么条件？What are the estimated costs, potential credits or funding, and the conditions attached?

投入与收益Effort and benefits

需要多久、需要我们配合什么？有哪些收益和风险？How long would it take, what would our team need to contribute, and what are the benefits and risks?

「请把您的建议整理成一份简短方案，我们会带回内部比较，并跟进需要确认的部分。」“Could you put your recommendation into a short proposal? We will review it internally and follow up on any open points.”
会议观察与我的跟进建议MEETING OBSERVATIONS & PROPOSED FOLLOW-UP

把讨论整理成可跟进的事项

Turn the discussion into clear follow-up actions
